About Morrison Ranch

What Morrison Ranch is, and why people fall for it.

Morrison Ranch is a master-planned community on the east side of Gilbert, generally bordered by Higley and Elliot Roads (zip codes 85296 and 85234). The look is unmistakable — tree-lined streets, white ranch-style fencing, deep front porches, gas-style lamp posts, greenbelts, catch-and-release lakes, and the water towers that mark the community from a distance.

It was built with a New England farmhouse sensibility and a clear intention: to feel like a real neighborhood, where evenings are slow, kids play outside until the streetlights come on, and neighbors actually know each other. I moved my own family here for exactly that reason, and it's why I tell clients it's the best neighborhood in Gilbert — not as a sales line, but because I live it every day.

Parks & Greenbelts

Lakes, trails, and a lot of reasons to be outside.

Morrison Ranch was laid out around its greenbelts, not around them. Catch-and-release fishing lakes anchor several villages, connected by miles of walking and biking trails, ramadas, and picnic areas that thread through the whole community.

Playgrounds are spaced so most homes are a short walk from one, and the trail system makes it realistic to bike to a friend's house, a park, or the Heritage District without ever touching a main road. It's the single biggest reason families tell me they chose Morrison Ranch over another Gilbert neighborhood.

Schools

Family-first, and it shows.

Morrison Ranch is served by highly regarded Gilbert-area public schools, primarily within Gilbert Public Schools and Higley Unified School District. Because the community spans multiple villages, exact school assignment depends on the specific home's address — I always confirm current boundaries for whatever property a client is considering, since they can shift.

Strong schools are one of the top reasons families put down roots here, and it shows up in how many of my clients are repeat Morrison Ranch families — moving from one village to another as their kids grow, rather than leaving the neighborhood.

HOA Information

What the HOA actually covers.

Morrison Ranch has a homeowners association that maintains the greenbelts, lakes, common landscaping, and shared amenities, and organizes community events throughout the year — food-truck nights, seasonal gatherings, and neighborhood get-togethers.

Dues vary by village and by whether a home sits in a sub-association, so I always pull the current amount and any special assessments for the specific address, rather than quoting a single number for the whole community.

Buying in Morrison Ranch

Buying here well starts with the village, not the listing.

The spread between Morrison Ranch's entry price and its top end is bigger than most buyers expect — which is exactly why I always start with the village and the lot, not just the number on the listing. Different villages hold value differently, and the streets that back to greenbelt or lake front tend to move the fastest.

If you're relocating from out of state, this is where having a local guide actually matters. I'd rather walk you through which village fits your life first, then go look at homes — not the other way around.

Location & Commute

Central to everything the East Valley offers.

Morrison Ranch sits minutes from the Loop 202 (Santan Freeway) and US-60, making the rest of the East Valley and greater Phoenix easy to reach — roughly 30–40 minutes to Phoenix Sky Harbor Airport. It's the rare community that feels tucked away but stays connected.

Frequently Asked

Morrison Ranch questions, answered.

Where is Morrison Ranch located?

On the east side of Gilbert, Arizona, generally around Higley and Elliot Roads (zip codes 85296 and 85234), minutes from the Loop 202 and US-60.

What are the villages of Morrison Ranch?

Several distinct villages, including Lakeview Trails (North & South), Higley Groves, The Orchards, Elliot Groves, and Warner Groves — each with its own feel, home age, and price range.

What do homes cost in Morrison Ranch?

As of 2026, generally the high $500,000s to $1.3 million+, depending on the village, lot, size, and condition. Turnkey homes in the best villages sell quickly.

What schools serve Morrison Ranch?

Highly rated Gilbert-area public schools, primarily within Gilbert Public Schools and Higley Unified School District. Assignment depends on the exact address.

Does Morrison Ranch have an HOA?

Yes — it maintains the greenbelts, lakes, and common areas and hosts community events. Dues vary by village, so confirm the current amount for the specific home.

What's nearby for dining and shopping?

Agritopia and Joe's Farm Grill, Downtown Gilbert's Heritage District, and SanTan Village are all a short drive away.

How do I buy a home in Morrison Ranch?

Start with the village, not the listing — get pre-approved, and work with someone who knows the streets, not just the neighborhood name.

How do I sell my home in Morrison Ranch?

Price it off what's selling in your specific village, not a Gilbert-wide average, and market it to buyers already searching for this lifestyle by name.

Is Morrison Ranch a good place to live?

It's one of the most sought-after communities in Gilbert — loved for its look, its schools, its family-friendly amenities, and its central East Valley location.
